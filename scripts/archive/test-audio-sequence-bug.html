🔍 Root Cause Analysis

The bug: When startSeq=1 is passed, the current code does startIndex = 1 - 1 = 0, then later at line 115 it does currentSeq = 0 + 1 = 1. This is correct!

BUT WAIT: There's a subtle interaction bug. Let me trace through what happens when you play "hello" (word 1):

  1. handlePlayWord calls streamer.StreamWord(ctx, client, sessionID, "hello", 0)
  2. The bug is in the sequence numbering, not the start index

Actual Bug: Look at streamer.go line 115: currentSeq := uint32(i + 1)

When you have 5 words and play word 1 ("hello"), the streamer sends sequences 1,2,3... for "hello". But if there's any buffering or cross-contamination, sequences from other words might mix in!

🎯 Real Bug: Sequence Number Reuse

Hypothesis: The sequence counter is not per-word, it's global or reused. When multiple words are generated in a session, their audio frames might have overlapping or conflicting sequence numbers.
