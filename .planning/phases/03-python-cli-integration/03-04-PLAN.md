## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-03-14 | Information Disclosure | --file 临时文件 | low | mitigate | reviewArgs 大负载写 os.CreateTemp("", "hta-review-*.json")（系统临时目录，权限受限），cleanup func 立即 os.Remove；临时文件含 word/rating 但不含密钥 |
